On Day 17b (2026-10-07) the Master Thread wrote two questions into the
designer's handover: what a second press on a chosen mood word does, and the
wording of a failed-save sentence. Umm E Habiba corrected it: "these are mine
to decide". She was right, and she answered both at once when they were put
to her as short choices with a recommendation.

**Why:** what the product does and says is the owner's decision. The
designer draws what was decided. Sending him a product question delays it
and hands away a decision she wants to make.

**How to apply:** before adding a question to `docs/ui-handover.md`, sort
it. How something looks or where it sits is the designer's. What happens
when a control is pressed, and what a sentence says, is hers: ask her as a
short choice with the cost of each option, then tell the designer the
answer as a decision. My interpretation: layout questions with a product
consequence should go to her first too. See [[neuron-teaching-mode]].
