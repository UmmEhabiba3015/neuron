Stated by Umm E Habiba on 2026-10-08 (Day 18 opening), after the Master Thread
explained a missing "Signed-in devices" section and recommended an option:

1. **She or her husband decides the feature boundary.** Not the Master Thread,
   not the designer. Present scope as a choice with costs; a recommendation is
   fine but must be labelled as advice, never acted on as settled.
2. **Text in the designs (`designs/AIJournal-v3/`) is placeholder.** It shows
   where words go, not what they must say or what the product must do. Do not
   cite design copy as a requirement.

**Why:** she owns the product; earlier sessions treated design copy and
Master-Thread judgement as if they set scope.

**How to apply:** when a design shows something unbuilt, say what the API can
and cannot do and ask her. Never say "the design says X so we do X". Extends
[[neuron-product-decisions-are-hers]].
