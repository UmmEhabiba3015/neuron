Direction from the project owner on 2026-09-21: **every day opens with an
overview of the whole day before the first block starts.** State how many blocks
there are, what each one covers in a line, which are decisions versus
implementation, and where a split would fall if the day is heavy.

She asked for this after several days where the block count only emerged when
she asked mid-session ("how many blocks are for today", "how many topics are in
that day").

**Why:** she has roughly 7 focused hours a day and needs to judge at the start
whether a day fits, rather than discovering the size at block three. It also
lets her decide the scope — full day, split, or stop early — while that decision
is still cheap.

**How to apply:** open with a compact table or list, then start block one. This
does not replace the short brief in [[neuron-teaching-mode]] — it sharpens it:
the brief says what the day is for, the overview says how big it is. Still one
block at a time after that; the overview is a map, not a licence to dump the
whole day's content up front.
