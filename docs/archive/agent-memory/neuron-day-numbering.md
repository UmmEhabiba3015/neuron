Neuron was a public build on LinkedIn, with posts numbered from **Day 0** (2026-07-27, repo init). Days inserted into the old plan took letters (17a, 17b, 17c) or names (Screens Day). The day-numbered plan ended at Screens Day; work is now by feature ([[neuron-feature-plan]]).

**On 2026-10-13 her husband stopped the LinkedIn posts.** No posts while building. When the project is finished it will be presented in public feature by feature, because he found the earlier posts weak.

**Why:** the posts were not worth the effort, and presenting finished features tells a better story.

**How to apply:** talk about work as "Feature N, block M". Use day numbers only when citing history (old reports and ADR amendments say "Day 17b" and so on). Do not prepare LinkedIn material unless asked; at each feature's close, record what was built, trade-offs, mistakes and lessons in `master-state.md` for the later presentation.
