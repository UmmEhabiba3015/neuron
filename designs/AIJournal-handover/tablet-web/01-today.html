Stage 4, screen 1. The same screen as mobile, reflowed, not redesigned. Same markup, same order, same stylesheet.
The tablet is where the whole page fits on the screen. At 390 the graph runs off every edge. Here the page is 560px, exactly 40 graph modules, so its ruling lands on its own edge; the binding stays welded to its left, and 125px of unruled cream sits either side. The paper margin doubles to 28. The module stays 14.
No second pane, and the reason is the flow rather than the width. Timeline's two zooms are one screen behind a control, and a Timeline standing beside Today would make the word destination meaningless.
The user's words go from 33 characters a line to 54, and the note follows them. The note was first frozen at 241px here, which left it stopping three fifths of the way across a row whose fill runs the full width. The rule is a ceiling, not a width: the note may not run wider than the writing. It is carried by size, colour, a labelled row and being shut until asked for, and not by measure.
The masthead is one row instead of two, which buys back 44px of vertical space, and it is still the same height on every destination, so the destination row does not move when you switch.
