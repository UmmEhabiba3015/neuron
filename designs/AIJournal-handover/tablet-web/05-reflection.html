Weekly reflection . Pro . tablet 834
Every row here is the product talking, so this file is nothing but notes. Frozen at 32ch they all stopped at 241px inside a 504px sheet and the screen read as half-loaded text. Subordination is carried by size, by rust, by the labelled key on every row and by the single correction control at the foot. Never by measure.
The only thing the product ever initiates, on a cadence the user consented to, which is the whole reason it is allowed to exist. The consent is restated at the foot with a way to change it, so it can never feel like something that started happening to you.
Three observations, all verifiable from the record, all inside the confidence tiers: two counts and one question. No cause is claimed. No score, no week over week comparison, no streak, no colour. Held short on purpose, because this is the surface most likely to drift into advice.
