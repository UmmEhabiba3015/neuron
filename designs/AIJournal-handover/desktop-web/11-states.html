2 . Day seven, already with a gap . AND IT DRAWS A ROW THE OTHER PLATFORMS DO NOT
The weakest week in the product. Four rows in seven days, three days with nothing. The silent days are not rows, are not counted, and have no language attached to them anywhere. In the index they are a number and nothing else: no box, no dot, no dimming that reads as failure, and no tap target, because there is nothing to go to.
The fourth row is Saturday's recording, and its absence at 390 and 834 is a content fault. The total says 3 entries and 1 recording; the list on the other two platforms shows three written rows and no recording at all. That is invisible there and impossible here, because the index has to mark the days that have something in them, and an index toning no audio day beside a total counting a recording is one screen giving two accounts of the record. Section 8.0 specifies this state as four rows and three unmarked days, which is exactly what is drawn: 3, 5, 8 and 9 marked, 4, 6 and 7 unmarked. Reported on the signed platforms rather than changed there.
The total is small and it is still only rising. No streak, no percentage, no prompt to do better, no red. This state has to feel like a thin notebook rather than a failed one.

4 . A recording on free, with no transcript
The head alone: time, play, waveform, duration. Nothing is blurred, greyed, locked or padlocked, and there is no upgrade button on the row.
The head carries its time on both tiers, which closed section 9.12 at session 6 and is rule 4: times in a fixed 58px left column. This state is the one that raised it, because Free has no transcript body for the time to sit in, and the row was carrying no time at all while every entry around it did. And it draws today's recording, 14:05 and 2:41: it used to draw a 5:08, which is Saturday the 8th's, on a sheet headed Today.
This is the single most important restraint in the product. An untranscribed memo is not a feature being withheld, it is work nobody has paid for, and the moment this row is drawn as a locked object the entire free tier becomes a demo.

5 . The product's note, open
Shut everywhere else. Open, the label swaps to Hide the note, the chevron flips, and the claim appears below a dotted rule with its citation and a way to mark it wrong.
Built with details and summary, so it needs no script, and keyboard operation, focus and expanded state all come from the browser rather than from anything reimplemented.
The open state is not the state fix 2c touched. That fix centres the printed key and the summary in a SHUT row, which is only as tall as the key itself; here the key labels a long block from its top, which is what the padding was written for.

6 . The crisis resource
Appears after the entry is complete. Never mid composition, never on open, never as a push, never in the timeline, never paywalled at any tier.
The same keyline card as every other static card. No colour, no icon, no alarm, no legal register. A warm thing that abruptly becomes a red banner is abandonment at the exact moment it matters most. It carries a way to say that isn't what this was, because false positives on grief, venting and fiction are guaranteed.
Documented shortfall, not a solved problem: on free this is a curated phrase list with no model behind it, and it does not cover voice memos at all.

8 . Guest, and the keep this moment . AND THE THIRD APPEARANCE OF ONE BUG
Not on arrival. The panel appears only after the first thing is written, so the user is being offered to keep something that exists rather than to insure an empty house.
A panel, not a modal: it pushes a history entry and Back closes it. Not yet is a quiet control and stays fully legible, because there is no disabled state and no guilt copy anywhere in this product.
This state found the same bug for the third time. The panel is positioned against the app rather than the page, which is invisible at 390 where the two are the same rectangle, needed the tablet's centring arithmetic at 834, and put the panel 68px inside one edge of the page and 428px short of the other at 1440. lock.css revision 44. Anything positioned against .app is suspect at every new platform, and this is now the third platform to prove it.

10 . The composer, mid-draft
This state did not exist until the flow walk found that the typed path could not be completed. The composer was a field, a record control and the out-of-memory option, with no way to commit what you had typed.
One control, two states. The single filled control is the record control while the field is empty and the commit control the moment it is not. Same height, same fill.
The commit control is a word, not a glyph. It was drawn first as an arrow onto a baseline and the first person to look at it read it as download, which is the one thing it must not mean. An icon that has to be decoded is worse than a word that cannot be, and this product already speaks in printed labels: Upgrade, Export, Not now.
The field also stops being a placeholder here, so the text is ink rather than the quieter printed grey. The cost, accepted: the mic is unreachable mid-draft, so someone who starts typing and then wants to record has to clear the field.
