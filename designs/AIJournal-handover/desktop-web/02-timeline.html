Timeline . the record and its index . Free . desktop web 1440
Two zooms become one screen. At 390 and 834 the list and the calendar are alternatives behind a segmented control, because only one fits. At 1440 both fit, so the list is the record and the calendar is an index into it: every marked day is a link to that day's rows. The zoom control has nothing left to switch and is not on the screen.
The list gets the larger share by construction, not by eye. Its column is 560, the width the record has been read at since tablet, and the index takes what is left. The day cells come out at 48.6 square, which matters more here than it ever has: a cell used to be a link to a day page and is now the control that drives the screen.
The date box replaces the zoom control, so all four destinations finally compose their title block identically, which is what revision 27 was aiming at when it moved Ask's search field out of the masthead. Nothing above the destination row moves when you switch: measured at 138 on both.
An empty day is still a number and nothing else, and is not a link, because there is nothing to go to. A fragment link pushes a history entry, which the flow deliberately avoids for zoom, so the build must scroll without pushing.
