2 . The first Pro session . the only demonstration in the product
It leads with their own words. A sentence they spoke in March, never read back, in ink at body size, dated. The observation comes second, smaller, in rust, in a labelled row, with its citation and a way to mark it wrong.
The obvious move is to open with what the product noticed, because that is the capability being sold. It is wrong. Leading with the observation makes the moment about the product; leading with their voice makes it about them, which is the thesis of the whole brief.
And it stays unexcited. No confetti, no welcome to Pro, no you're all set, no exclamation mark. The register that makes this safe to open at 1am does not get suspended because money changed hands. The product just starts doing what it said.
