The tab bar is four printed words, not four icons. The current destination keeps the filled ground and inverted label it has on every other platform, because iOS marks the current tab by tint alone and rule 2.4 forbids colour carrying information on its own. You is still set apart, by a hairline rather than by the auto margin the web platforms use: a tab bar divides equally, so the space that says it at 390 and 834 is not available, and the rule says the same thing in the product's own vocabulary.
